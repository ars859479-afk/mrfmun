01 / The conference

More than a
conference.

A room full of perspectives. A table full of possibilities. A platform to turn curiosity into leadership.

“Every great resolution begins with someone willing to speak first.”

MRFMUN brings students together to explore international affairs, practise diplomacy, and challenge one another's ideas in a structured, respectful setting.

Delegates represent countries, negotiate with fellow participants, write working papers, and work toward resolutions. Whether this is your first conference or your next challenge, come prepared to listen, question, collaborate, and lead.

Our inaugural dispatch is a call to do the reading, bring an open mind, and leave the room with a broader view of the world.

01 — Research

Know your portfolio, understand the issue, and build your argument on credible sources.

02 — Diplomacy

Challenge ideas, not people. Listen actively and negotiate with purpose.

03 — Leadership

Speak with clarity, work across differences, and help turn debate into action.

One experience.

A preview of the rhythm of conference. A detailed schedule and timings will be shared closer to the event.

Arrival & Opening

Welcome, opening ceremony, committee orientation, and the first formal session. Meet your fellow delegates and set your priorities.

04 / Before you arrive

Come prepared.
Leave changed.

The strongest delegate is curious, well-prepared, and ready to work with people who see the world differently.

  • Research your assigned country or portfolio.
  • Read the committee mandate and topic background.
  • Prepare a position paper and opening statement.
  • Bring a notebook, pen, and an open mind.
  • Practise respectful debate and collaborative problem-solving.

Delegate advisory

Keep an eye out for official updates about registration, committee agendas, position-paper requirements, dress code, and venue guidance. Don't rely on unofficial timings or draft information; follow the Secretariat's announcements for confirmed details.
